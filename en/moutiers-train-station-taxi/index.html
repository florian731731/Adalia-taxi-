Moûtiers – Salins – Brides-les-Bains is the rail gateway to the 3 Vallées. Every winter it welcomes direct TGV inOui services from Paris, Lille and Nantes, daily OUIGO trains from Paris between December and March (around 5 hours), and the international Eurostar services from Amsterdam, Brussels and Lille heading to Bourg-Saint-Maurice. Regional TER trains connect Chambéry and Bourg-Saint-Maurice all year round.

Adalia Taxi is based in Moûtiers, minutes from the station: we meet you as you step off the train and drive you without any change to Courchevel, Méribel, Val Thorens, Les Menuires, Brides-les-Bains, Valmorel or La Plagne — right to your chalet or hotel door, skis and luggage included.

About Moûtiers station

Opened on 1 June 1893 when the line was extended from Albertville, Moûtiers – Salins – Brides-les-Bains station sits at 480 m on the Tarentaise line (Saint-Pierre-d'Albigny – Bourg-Saint-Maurice). It is now the main rail access point for the 3 Vallées, with a large adjoining bus station for connections to the resorts.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I meet my driver at Moûtiers station?

At the main exit, on the station forecourt. We confirm the meeting point by text message and can wait with a name board on request.

What happens if my train is delayed?

We track your train's actual arrival time and your driver adjusts accordingly — nothing for you to do.

Can I go straight from the station to Courchevel, Méribel or Val Thorens?

Yes, with no change: allow 25-35 min to Courchevel, 20-25 min to Méribel and 45-55 min to Val Thorens depending on traffic and road conditions.

Will my skis and luggage fit?

Yes, the vehicle is equipped for skis, snowboards and suitcases. Just tell us the number of passengers and bags when you book.

Do I need to book for a Saturday in winter?

Strongly recommended: it is the busiest day of the week. Book as soon as you buy your ticket, with your train number and arrival address. No prices are published on this site: quotes are free and confirmed before you book.
