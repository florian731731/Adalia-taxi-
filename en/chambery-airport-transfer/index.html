Discover Chambéry Airport

Opened in the 1930s as a mixed civil and military airfield, Chambéry-Savoie Airport developed its commercial activity from 1960. It has since specialised in winter charter flights from the United Kingdom, making it one of the main air gateways to the ski resorts of Savoie and Haute-Savoie during the season. Its closeness to the Tarentaise valley makes it a favourite option to reach the slopes quickly.
