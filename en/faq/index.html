Booking & rates

How do I request a quote?

Call 06 35 40 59 62 for an immediate answer, or fill in our online form with your route and times: we'll get back to you within 1 hour with a free, no-obligation quote.

Do I need to book the taxi in advance?

It's not compulsory, but it's recommended during busy periods (school holidays, winter season) to guarantee immediate pickup. The quote is free and with no obligation.

How is the fare calculated?

The fare follows the taxi rate regulated by the Savoie prefecture, applied via the meter. No prices are published on this site: we give you a free, precise quote before booking, with no surprises on arrival.

Is Adalia Taxi available at night, on weekends and public holidays?

Yes, the service runs 24/7, including nights, weekends and public holidays, for transfers to train stations, airports and ski resorts.

Service area

Does Adalia Taxi cover the whole Tarentaise valley?

Yes. Based in Moûtiers, Adalia Taxi provides transfers to all Tarentaise ski resorts (Trois Vallées, Paradiski, Espace Killy...), as well as to train stations and airports across the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region and Geneva.

What is Adalia Taxi's service area?

Adalia Taxi is licensed in the commune of Moûtiers and covers the whole Tarentaise valley: Albertville, Courchevel, Méribel, Val Thorens, Tignes, Val d'Isère and Bourg-Saint-Maurice, as well as train stations and airports across the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region and Geneva.

Can I book a taxi to the airport or train station from Moûtiers?

Yes, Adalia Taxi offers direct transfers to train stations (Chambéry, Albertville, Bourg-Saint-Maurice, Lyon Part-Dieu) and airports (Lyon-Saint Exupéry, Geneva, Chambéry, Grenoble, Annecy).

Can you pick me up in Albertville?

Yes. Our taxi licence is registered in the commune of Moûtiers, but with advance booking we'll pick you up anywhere in Albertville — town centre, train station, Albertville-Moûtiers Hospital (CHAM) — and take you to your destination, just like a regular taxi.

Is there Uber in Moûtiers?

Ride-hailing app coverage is very limited in the Tarentaise valley, especially at night, on weekends and in the resorts. A local taxi like Adalia Taxi remains the most reliable way to be picked up on time, whether at Moûtiers station or in the resorts.

Specific services

Is medical transport covered?

Adalia Taxi provides transport to your medical appointments, compatible with CPAM (French health insurance) reimbursement on medical prescription.

Do you offer school transport?

Yes, for families throughout the Tarentaise valley, with regular year-round trips (morning and evening) or one-off trips as needed.
