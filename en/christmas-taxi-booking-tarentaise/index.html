Book Your Taxi for the Christmas Holidays in Tarentaise

Practical tips for booking your taxi in time between Moûtiers train station, the region's airports and the Trois Vallées ski resorts, before the season rush.

Available 24/7All of Tarentaise coveredLicensed driverFree quote within 1 hour

Every year, the Christmas and New Year holidays are the busiest period of the season in Tarentaise: tens of thousands of holidaymakers arrive and leave within just a few days, mostly between 20 and 27 December for arrivals, and around 1-3 January for departures. Moûtiers and Bourg-Saint-Maurice train stations, as well as Geneva, Chambéry and Lyon airports, all see heavy traffic during this window.

Unlike a large taxi company with dozens of vehicles, Adalia Taxi operates with a single car. That means a personal service with a driver who gets to know you, but it also means a limited number of trips can be handled on the busiest days. That's why booking early makes a real difference.

Our booking tips

Book as soon as your train or flight tickets are confirmed
Share your exact schedule as early as your first message
Book both outbound and return trips in one go
Let us know passenger numbers and luggage (skis included)
Flights and trains tracked live, no charge for a short delay

The busiest periods

Besides the Christmas holidays (20 December to 3 January), the February half-term holidays are also very busy in Tarentaise, with a peak around the weekends when school zones change over. If your stay falls within one of these windows, it's best to get in touch several weeks ahead rather than booking at the last minute.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book my taxi for the Christmas holidays?

As soon as your train or flight tickets are confirmed, ideally several weeks ahead, especially for arrivals or departures between 20 December and 3 January, the busiest period of the season in Tarentaise.

Are rates higher during the school holidays?

No prices are published in advance on this site: every trip is quoted individually, free of charge and communicated before you book, whatever the period.

What happens if my train or flight is delayed on the day?

Adalia Taxi tracks your train or flight in real time and adjusts the pickup time accordingly, at no extra charge for a short delay.

Can I book my outbound and return trip at the same time?

Yes, and it's recommended: mention both trips in your first message to secure both time slots, especially on the busiest dates.

Do you take bookings for groups or large families?

Yes, just let us know the number of passengers and luggage (skis included) when you book so we can plan your trip accordingly.
