Geneva Airport Transfer to Moûtiers

Comfortable private taxi transfer between Geneva Airport and Moûtiers or your Tarentaise ski resort. English-speaking service.

Distance: about 160 km • Travel time: around 1h45–2h from Moûtiers

Available 24/7All of the Tarentaise valleyLicensed driverFree quote within 1 hour

Geneva Airport is a preferred option for many international travellers heading to the Tarentaise ski resorts. Adalia Taxi offers a comfortable, stress-free transfer for this longer journey, with a driver used to this regular route.

The route crosses part of Haute-Savoie before reaching the Tarentaise valley — expect a longer drive than from a local airport, which we let you know in advance so you can plan your arrival with peace of mind.
