Frequently asked questions

Your questions about taxis in Moûtiers

Do you serve all the Tarentaise ski resorts?

Yes. Based in Moûtiers, Adalia Taxi provides transfers to every resort in the Trois Vallées and Espace Killy ski areas, as well as to regional train stations and airports (Geneva, Lyon, Chambéry, Grenoble, Annecy).

Can I book an airport transfer to or from Geneva or Lyon?

Yes, direct transfers are available to Geneva Airport and Lyon-Saint Exupéry Airport, with flight tracking included.

Is there room for ski equipment and luggage?

Yes, our vehicles are equipped to carry skis, snowboards and luggage at no extra charge.

How do I get a quote?

The simplest way is to call us directly at +33 6 35 40 59 62, or fill in the contact form with your route and schedule.

What is the best taxi in Moûtiers?

Adalia Taxi is a licensed taxi based in Moûtiers, rated 5.0/5 on Google with 30 customer reviews, available 24/7 across the whole Tarentaise valley.

Is Adalia Taxi available at night, on weekends and public holidays?

Yes, the service operates 24/7, including nights, weekends and public holidays, for transfers to train stations, airports and ski resorts.

Do I need to book the taxi in advance?

It's not required, but it's recommended during busy periods (school holidays, winter season) to guarantee immediate availability. Quotes are free with no obligation.

What is Adalia Taxi's service area?

Adalia Taxi is licensed in the town of Moûtiers and covers the whole Tarentaise valley: Albertville, Courchevel, Méribel, Val Thorens, Tignes, Val d'Isère and Bourg-Saint-Maurice, as well as train stations and airports across the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes region and Geneva.
